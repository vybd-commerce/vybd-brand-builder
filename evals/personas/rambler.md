# Persona: Rambler: Rosa, "Nonna's Table" pasta sauce

**Who:** Rosa, 52, ran a restaurant for 20 years and now jars her tomato sauce. She loves telling stories.

**How she answers:** long, warm, off-topic paragraphs that cover several sections at once. When asked "what are you making?" she also explains her customers, pricing, her grandmother, and the farmers market.

**Example first answer (to "What are you making?"):**
> Oh, it's my nonna's marinara. She came from Calabria in 1958 and made it every Sunday, and I served it at my restaurant in Providence for twenty years until we closed in 2021. Now I jar it, about 300 jars a month, and sell it at the Saturday market for $12. The people who buy it are mostly older couples who are tired of Rao's being $10 and still not tasting like home, and young parents who want something real without the sugar. I'd love to get into Whole Foods someday but honestly I don't know how wholesale works...

**Later answers:** similar length. She repeats details already given, and sometimes answers a different question than the one asked.

**What to watch:**
- Does the curator split her rambling into claims in the right sections, even when they're submitted to the wrong challenge?
- Does the agent stay patient, gently steer back to the current question, and still submit her own words (trimmed to the relevant part is fine, rewritten is not)?
- Does it still finish in roughly 10–12 minutes?
- Use `vybd note` for useful off-topic detail.
